# Roadmap

> עברית: מפת הדרכים של GOG Enhancer בקצרה (באנגלית). אין התחייבות לתאריכים.

A status list for **GOG Enhancer**, an unofficial helper for gog.com. It shows what has shipped and what is still open. There are no dates, and items can change or be dropped. The detailed history is in the extension's in-app "What's new" panel.

## Shipped

- **2.5** — Lowest-price badge, stale-rate warning with the exact rate, tag-colour dot on store cards, shareable tag packs.
- **2.6** — Genre-aware card styling read from the game page.
- **2.7** — Safer settings import, exact storage usage, reliability and test work.
- **2.8** — Command palette (Ctrl/⌘ + K), wishlist-wide price alerts, play status, auto theme, seven interface languages, options search, undo toast.
- **2.9** — Wishlist deals, "worth waiting?" verdict, wishlist value, type-checked code.
- **3.0** — CheapShark price table and deal score, sale calendar, encrypted backup, spending tracker, achievements, custom theme.
- **4.0** — Rebuilt for gog.com's redesign behind one site adapter; library learning and pack contents; folder sync; privacy dashboard; notification pipeline; opt-in endless scrolling; calendar files; keyboard access.

## Open

- **Copy-price button** on converted prices.
- **Refund-date auto-fill** from order history (purchases are still logged by hand).
- **More resilient mods scraping**, with a visible error when the page layout changes.
- **Dependency updates** for development tools.

## Partly done

- **Lowest price ever, anywhere** — the cross-store comparison and deal score exist, but a true all-time low across stores is not shown.
- **Bundle and DLC value** — a pack's real contents and overlap with games you own are shown, but not a total against the bundle price.
- **Regional price comparison** — prices can be shown in several currencies, but there is no "cheaper in another region" comparison yet.

## Not planned

- Anything that needs your GOG account password or sends your library to a server. The extension stays local-first.

Ideas and bug reports are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/DrummingBird1/GOGEnhancer/issues).
